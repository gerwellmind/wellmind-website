Refund policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Not happy? Get your money back. If you are not happy with the Well Mind app, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying. No questions, even if you have already used it.

How to ask

Email ger@wellmind.ie with the name and email used at checkout (or forward your Stripe receipt). We will refund the full amount you paid to the original payment method, usually within 5 working days. Your bank may take 5 to 10 days to show it.

Gifts

Gift purchases can be refunded to the person who paid, within 14 days of buying. Once a gift is refunded, the gift link may stop working.

After 14 days

If the app has a fault or doesn't work as described, you still have your legal rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 to have it fixed, a price reduction, or a refund. Just email us.
