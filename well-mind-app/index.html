BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few practical
questions.

Something else? Ask Ger

Do I need to download it from an app store?

No. Open wellmind.ie/app on your phone, tablet or computer. On a phone you can add it to your home screen so it opens like any other app: in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen; in Chrome tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen.

Where is my journal stored? Is it private?

Your entries stay on your own device, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share something with Ger by email. Use “Back up my journal” in the app now and then, so you can restore it on a new phone.

What do I get for free?

The morning and evening check-ins, the meditations, the calm tools, reminders, four complete chapters and a preview of every other chapter. Full access is a one-off €30 and includes all future updates.

I’m a WellMind client. Do I need to pay?

No. The full app is included with WellMind personal development sessions. Ask Ger for your code and enter it in the app under “Working with Ger?”.

Can steps and sleep sync from my phone or watch?

Not at the moment — you type your steps in the evening check-in, which takes a few seconds. Your phone’s Health or Fit app shows the number.

Is this therapy?

No. Well Mind is for adult education, reflection and everyday personal development. It does not provide a diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for professional care. If you feel unsafe or at risk, contact 112 / 999 or the Samaritans on 116 123.

I paid but the app is still locked.

Email ger@wellmind.ie with the name and email you used to pay, and we’ll unlock it for you. Please do not email your card details.
