Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

WellMind is run by Gerard Boland, trading as WellMind (Well Mind), 74 Burrin Manor, Carlow, Ireland. Email: ger@wellmind.ie.

We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

2. Your journal stays on your device

Everything you write or tap in the Well Mind app (check-ins, feelings, notes, chapter answers) is saved only in your browser on your own device. It is not sent to us and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your browser.

3. What we do collect, and why

  • When you buy: Stripe processes your payment. We receive your name, email address, the amount, and the date. We use this to provide your purchase, give refunds and keep accounts (legal basis: contract and legal obligation). We keep these records for 6 years, as Irish tax law requires. We never see your full card details.
  • When you email us: we use your email and message to reply (legitimate interest), and keep it no longer than needed.
  • Website statistics: we use privacy-friendly statistics that do not use cookies and do not identify you, to count visits and purchases.
  • Technical logs: our website host (GitHub Pages) records IP addresses for security. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
  • Meditation videos: these are YouTube videos shown in privacy-enhanced mode. When you press play, YouTube (Google) processes data under its own privacy policy.

4. Cookies

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage only to save your own journal and access on your device, which is strictly necessary for the app to work.

5. Who we share data with

Only the service providers above: Stripe (payments), GitHub (hosting), Google (fonts, YouTube) and our statistics provider. Some are based in the US; transfers are protected by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses. We never sell your data.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict its use, or receive a copy of it. Email ger@wellmind.ie. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

7. Age

Well Mind is intended for adults. Under-16s should use it only with a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We will update this page if anything changes, and change the date at the top.
