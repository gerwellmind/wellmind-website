Something else? Ask Ger

When do I get the gift card?

Straight away. After paying you go to your gift card page, where you add their name and a message, then print it, save it as a PDF or send the link. Bookmark that page if you want to come back to it.

Can I give it before Christmas Day?

Yes — the card works whenever they open it. Print it and wrap it, or send it on Christmas morning.

How do they open their gift?

They scan the QR code with their phone camera, or tap the link. Well Mind opens with everything unlocked. They can then add it to their home screen.

Is this therapy?

No. Well Mind is for adult education, reflection and everyday personal development. It isn’t a diagnosis or treatment, and it’s not a substitute for professional care.

I paid but lost the gift card.

Email ger@wellmind.ie with the name and email you paid with, and we’ll send you the gift link again.
