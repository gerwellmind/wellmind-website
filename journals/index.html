BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few practical
questions.

Need help choosing? Ask Ger

Is this a printed book or a download?

These offers are digital downloads. Each individual journal is a PDF; the bundle is a ZIP containing both journal PDFs and the weekly worksheet. No physical product is shipped.

Can I print the journals at home?

Yes. Print the pages you want for your own use. The journals use an 8 × 10-inch page size; choose “fit to page” for A4 or another paper size. Colour printing keeps the chapter colours, but you can use greyscale too.

Can I write in them on a phone or tablet?

The two journals are standard PDFs, with space for handwriting rather than fillable form fields. To write on screen, use a PDF app that supports annotation. The separate weekly worksheet in the bundle has fillable fields and can also be printed.

Do I need both journals?

No. Everyday Wellbeing focuses on everyday habits and body care; Understanding Yourself focuses on feelings, values and relationships. Both include the Wheel of Life and GROW framework, so either can be your starting point. Choose the bundle if you want both, plus the weekly worksheet.

Is there a programme or a deadline?

No. The journals are undated and self-paced. You can follow the chapters in order, choose a topic that matters now, or revisit an activity later.

Who are these journals for?

Adults who want approachable educational activities for reflection and everyday personal development. They do not provide a diagnosis, individual treatment or a substitute for professional care. You can choose which activities fit your needs.

What if I need help with my download?

Email ger@wellmind.ie with your order details and a description of the problem. Your Payhip receipt will contain the download link. Please do not email your payment-card details.
